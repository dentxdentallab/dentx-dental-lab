QUESTIONS FROM OFFICES

Before you
send a case.

How fast are zirconia and E.max crowns?

Zirconia crowns, E.max crowns and veneers take 5 business days. Layered zirconia or E.max takes 6 business days. Times are counted in business days and include pickup and dropoff.

Do you pick up and deliver cases?

Yes. Pickup of cases from your office and dropoff of completed work are included in every turnaround time.

Which intraoral scanners can send cases to DentX?

DentX accepts digital cases from Medit, iTero, Shining 3D and DEXIS IOS Cloud, or you can email STL files. Call the lab to connect your scanner account.

Is shade matching included?

Yes. DentX does complimentary shade matching for every client. Bring the shade tab or send a photo.

How long do dentures take?

Full dentures take 6 business days for the bite block, 7 for teeth try-in and 7 for the final finish. Printed dentures take 7 business days. Valplast finish takes 12 business days.

Is there a rush option?

Stayplates can be done in 2 business days with a $50 rush fee. Standard stayplate turnaround is 4 business days.
